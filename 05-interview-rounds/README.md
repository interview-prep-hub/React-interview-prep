# Interview Rounds - Practice Questions

This folder is where you practise on your own. There is one folder for each kind of round you may face. Most rounds have a **questions** file and an **answers** file with the same name and the same question IDs (for example `R2-HK-014`). Try a question first, say your answer out loud, then open the matching answer and mark yourself.

## The nine rounds

| # | Round | What it tests | Time to allow | Start here |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Screening quiz | Fast recall of React and JavaScript basics | 30 to 45 minutes | [Round 1](round-1-screening-quiz/README.md) |
| 2 | Concepts and theory | Can you explain how and why React works? | 45 to 60 minutes | [Round 2](round-2-concepts-and-theory/README.md) |
| 3 | Output prediction and debugging | Do you really know renders, closures and effects? | 30 to 45 minutes | [Round 3](round-3-output-and-debugging/README.md) |
| 4 | Machine coding | Build a small working UI from a spec | 45 to 90 minutes | [Round 4](round-4-machine-coding/README.md) |
| 5 | JavaScript coding | Utilities, polyfills, async code, small data structures | 20 to 40 minutes | [Round 5](round-5-javascript-coding/README.md) |
| 6 | Code review and refactoring | Can you spot problems and fix them? | 30 to 45 minutes | [Round 6](round-6-code-review-and-refactoring/README.md) |
| 7 | Performance and testing | Find slow code, write good tests | 45 minutes | [Round 7](round-7-performance-and-testing/README.md) |
| 8 | Frontend system design | Design a whole frontend feature or product | 45 to 60 minutes | [Round 8](round-8-frontend-system-design/README.md) |
| 9 | Behavioral and project | Your stories, your project, your questions | 30 to 45 minutes | [Round 9](round-9-behavioral-and-project/README.md) |

Rounds 4 and 5 do not have question files. Their questions are the runnable exercises in the [practice app](../practice-app/README.md), and the tests tell you when you are done.

## Suggested order

1. Learn a topic in the lessons, then do the matching part of Round 1 and Round 2 the next day.
2. Do Round 3 after the hooks lessons. It is the best test of whether you understand renders and effects.
3. Do a Round 4 exercise and a Round 5 exercise most days. Coding speed only comes from repetition.
4. Start Rounds 6 and 7 once the basics are solid.
5. Start Round 8 about two weeks before the interview (earlier for senior roles), and Round 9 one week before.

## How to run a mock round

1. Pick the round and a time limit from the table.
2. Set a timer. Close the answers file.
3. Answer out loud, as if someone were listening. Record yourself if you can.
4. When time ends, open the answers and score each item: **2** (complete and correct), **1** (partly right), **0** (wrong or blank).
5. Write the IDs of every item you scored 0 or 1 in a list. Redo them after one day, three days and seven days.

A score of 80% or more means you are ready for that round. Between 60% and 80%, review the linked lessons. Below 60%, go back to the lesson first and repeat the round later.

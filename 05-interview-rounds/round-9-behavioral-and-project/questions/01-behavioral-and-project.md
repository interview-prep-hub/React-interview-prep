# Round 9 - Behavioral and Project: Behavioral, Project Deep Dive and Questions to Ask

**How to use this file:** Pick a section, read one question, then answer it out loud (or record yourself) in 2 minutes for behavioral questions and 3 minutes for project questions. Use your own real stories, never invented ones. Then open the answers file, compare your structure with the model outline, and score yourself with the rubric in the [Round 9 README](../README.md). Do the HR and salary questions once, write your honest answers down, and re-read them before every recruiter call.

## Behavioral: introductions
**R9-BH-001 (Easy)** Walk me through your background. You have about two minutes.

**R9-BH-002 (Easy)** Why do you want to work at this company, and why frontend work?

## Behavioral: working with people
**R9-BH-003 (Medium)** Tell me about a conflict with a teammate. What happened and how did you handle it?

**R9-BH-004 (Medium)** Tell me about a time you disagreed with a designer. How did you reach a decision?

**R9-BH-005 (Medium)** A product manager asks for a feature by Friday, and you think it cannot be done well in that time. What do you do? Tell me about a real case.

**R9-BH-006 (Medium)** Tell me about working with a backend team on an API that did not fit your screen. How did you agree on the contract?

**R9-BH-007 (Medium)** Tell me about code review feedback you received that you disagreed with at first. What did you do?

**R9-BH-008 (Medium)** How do you give hard feedback in a code review? Give a real example.

**R9-BH-009 (Medium)** Tell me about a time you mentored a junior engineer.

**R9-BH-010 (Hard)** Tell me about a time you pushed back on a decision you thought was wrong, and then committed to the outcome.

**R9-BH-011 (Hard)** Tell me about a time you led a change without having formal authority over the people involved.

## Behavioral: delivery and ownership
**R9-BH-012 (Medium)** Tell me about something you drove from the idea to production. What did you own?

**R9-BH-013 (Medium)** Tell me about a tight deadline. How did you estimate the work and cut scope?

**R9-BH-014 (Medium)** Tell me about a task with unclear requirements. How did you move forward?

**R9-BH-015 (Medium)** How do you balance technical debt against new features? Give a real example.

**R9-BH-016 (Medium)** What is your biggest professional failure, and what did you learn from it?

**R9-BH-017 (Hard)** Tell me about a production bug you caused or fixed. What did the postmortem say?

**R9-BH-018 (Hard)** A frontend release is broken in production and users are complaining. Walk me through how you would handle the incident, and tell me about a real one if you have one.

## Behavioral: growth, quality and judgement
**R9-BH-019 (Easy)** Tell me about a time you had to learn a new tool or library quickly.

**R9-BH-020 (Easy)** How do you keep up with the React and frontend ecosystem without chasing every trend?

**R9-BH-021 (Medium)** How do you make sure the code you ship is good? Talk about tests, reviews, CI and monitoring.

**R9-BH-022 (Medium)** How do you use AI coding tools in your work, and how do you check what they give you?

**R9-BH-023 (Medium)** Tell me about a time you improved accessibility in a product.

**R9-BH-024 (Hard)** Tell me about a technical decision you now regret.

**R9-BH-025 (Medium)** Tell me about a time you were wrong and changed your mind.

## Project deep dive
**R9-PJ-001 (Medium)** Walk me through the architecture of your last project, from the user to the server.

**R9-PJ-002 (Easy)** What was your exact role on that project? What did you build yourself, and what did others build?

**R9-PJ-003 (Medium)** Why did you pick React and your main libraries for that project? What were the alternatives?

**R9-PJ-004 (Medium)** How did you handle state in that project? Where did each kind of state live?

**R9-PJ-005 (Medium)** How did you fetch, cache and update server data?

**R9-PJ-006 (Easy)** How did you organise folders, components and styling?

**R9-PJ-007 (Medium)** How did you test it? What did you choose not to test?

**R9-PJ-008 (Medium)** How did you measure performance? Give a before and after example.

**R9-PJ-009 (Hard)** What was the hardest bug you hit in that project? How did you find the cause?

**R9-PJ-010 (Medium)** If you started the project again today, what would you change?

**R9-PJ-011 (Medium)** How did you handle errors and monitoring in production?

**R9-PJ-012 (Medium)** How did you handle authentication and basic security on the frontend?

**R9-PJ-013 (Medium)** How did your code get from a pull request to production? Describe the pipeline.

**R9-PJ-014 (Hard)** Tell me about a migration or big upgrade you did (for example Create React App to Vite, JavaScript to TypeScript, or React 18 to 19). How did you keep the product working?

**R9-PJ-015 (Hard)** If your users, your data or your team grew ten times, what would break first in that project?

## Questions to ask the interviewer
**R9-QA-001 (Easy)** The interviewer says "Do you have any questions for us?" What is a good question about the first months in the role?

**R9-QA-002 (Easy)** What can you ask to learn how work is planned, reviewed and released?

**R9-QA-003 (Easy)** What can you ask to learn how the team keeps quality high?

**R9-QA-004 (Easy)** What can you ask about the frontend stack and its future?

**R9-QA-005 (Easy)** What can you ask about how design and engineering work together?

**R9-QA-006 (Medium)** What can you ask to learn the team's real challenges?

**R9-QA-007 (Medium)** What can you ask about incidents, on-call and what happens when production breaks?

**R9-QA-008 (Easy)** What can you ask about learning, feedback and growth?

**R9-QA-009 (Medium)** What is a strong closing question that lets you hear and fix the interviewer's doubts about you?

**R9-QA-010 (Easy)** What should you ask at the end about the hiring process itself?

## HR and salary questions
**R9-HR-001 (Easy)** What is your notice period, and can it be shortened?

**R9-HR-002 (Medium)** What are your salary expectations?

**R9-HR-003 (Medium)** Why are you leaving your current job?

**R9-HR-004 (Medium)** Do you have other offers, or are you interviewing elsewhere?

**R9-HR-005 (Medium)** Can you explain a gap in your CV or a very short stay at a job?

**R9-HR-006 (Easy)** Where do you see yourself in three to five years?

**R9-HR-007 (Easy)** Are you open to relocating, working on site, or working in a different time zone?

**R9-HR-008 (Medium)** What is your current salary?
